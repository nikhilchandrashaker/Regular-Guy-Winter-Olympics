One regular guy at the Winter Olympics

An average adult man enters 11 events across ice, snow and sliding sports. We simulate his performances thousands of times and see how far he is from gold.

Every event, as a share of gold-level performance

Press Play on any event to watch it. The gold marker is the benchmark. The bar is his median result; “best fluke” is his single best result across all simulations.

Gold benchmarks are rounded approximations of recent Olympic-level results. His baselines are guesses for an untrained adult who somehow gets onto the ice, slope or track, not measured data, and the random variation is a simple lognormal model.
